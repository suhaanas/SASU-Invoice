    Questions

    What is SASU Invoice?

    SASU Invoice is an online invoice generator for freelancers and small businesses. You fill in a short form, see the invoice build beside it, and download a clean, multi-page PDF. An optional cloud workspace adds client tracking, payment recording and automatic payment reminders.

    Is SASU Invoice free?

    Yes. The invoice editor is free forever with no account and no time limit. Paid plans (Pro at $12 a month and Business at $29 a month) are only for the cloud workspace, and every new workspace starts with a 14-day Pro trial with no card needed.

    Do I need an account to make an invoice?

    No. Open the editor, fill it in and download the PDF. You only create an account if you want invoices saved in the cloud, shared with your team, or followed up automatically.

    Where are my invoices stored?

    In the free editor, invoices are kept in your browser’s local storage on this device and never uploaded. Clearing your browser’s site data deletes them, so download a backup now and then. Invoices you save to a cloud workspace are stored on our servers in London.

    Can I use it on another device?

    Yes. Choose Download backup in the editor’s Your data panel, then use Import backup on the other device. With a cloud workspace your invoices are available on any device after you sign in.

    Can SASU Invoice chase unpaid invoices for me?

    Yes, with a cloud workspace. RecoverFlow sends polite payment reminders by email on the schedule you choose and stops as soon as you record the payment.

    Which currencies can I use?

    13 currencies, including US dollars, euros, pounds, rupees, yen and Sri Lankan rupees, each formatted the way that country expects.

    Can I make quotes and credit notes?

    Yes. Switch the document type to a quote, credit note, receipt or proforma. A quote turns into an invoice in one click, and a credit note links back to the invoice it corrects.

    Can I add my VAT or company number?

    Yes. Add your tax ID, your client’s tax ID and a legal footer such as a VAT exemption note.

    Does it send e-invoices for me?

    No. SASU Invoice is not a French approved e-invoicing platform (PA) or a Peppol access point, and it isn’t a legal archive, so keep your own copies. When e-invoicing becomes mandatory for your business, send through your platform or accountant.
