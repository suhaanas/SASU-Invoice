    PDFs that behave like documents

    Your client can select and search the text. Long invoices continue onto the next page with the table headers repeated and the pages numbered. Print and PNG export are one click away too.

    From blank page to paid in three steps

    1. Fill in the details

      Your business details are remembered and past clients are a click away. Pick payment terms and the due date works itself out.

    2. Watch it add up

      Every line you type updates the invoice: subtotal, discount, tax for each rate and the balance due.

    3. Send it

      Download a PDF or PNG, or print it. The invoice stays saved in your browser, so next month you can duplicate it and change the dates.

    Your invoices stay on your computer

    SASU Invoice has no accounts and no database. What you type is kept in your browser’s own storage and every export is generated on your device.

    To move to another computer, download a backup file from the editor and import it there.

    Why I built this

    I built SASU Invoice after one too many evenings fighting a spreadsheet template to send a two-line invoice. The tools I found wanted an account, a subscription, or my clients’ details on their servers.

    So this is the tool I wanted: open it, fill it in, and send a PDF that looks like it came from a proper business. No sign-up, and no copy of your data anywhere but your own browser.

    If something is missing or wrong, open an issue on GitHub. I read every one.

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There’s no paid plan, no trial and no account.

    Where are my invoices stored?

    In your browser’s local storage on this device. Clearing your browser’s site data deletes them, so download a backup now and then.

    Can I use it on another device?

    Yes. Choose Download backup in the editor’s Your data panel, then use Import backup on the other device.

    Is the Suggest button AI?

    No. It matches your words against a built-in library of professional phrasings. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

    Which currencies can I use?

    13 currencies, including US dollars, euros, pounds, rupees, yen and Sri Lankan rupees, each formatted the way that country expects.

    Can I add my VAT or company number?

    Yes. Add your tax ID, your client’s tax ID and a legal footer such as a VAT exemption note.
